# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

A business intelligence dashboard built with **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze e-commerce sales, profit, regional performance, customer/order activity, and shipping costs.

The dashboard converts e-commerce data into interactive business insights that can help identify profitable categories, regional performance differences, and areas where shipping costs may reduce profitability.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on answering practical business questions such as:

* How are total sales and profit performing?
* Which regions contribute the most to sales, profit, and orders?
* Which product categories generate stronger sales and profit?
* How much are shipping costs affecting profitability?
* Which categories have higher shipping costs per order or per unit?
* Where are potential opportunities to improve shipping efficiency?
* What are the main drivers behind overall profit?

The report is designed as an interactive Power BI dashboard with multiple analysis pages, slicers, KPI cards, charts, maps, and a decomposition tree.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses need to understand not only how much they sell, but also **where their profit comes from and which costs reduce profitability**.

The dashboard addresses this problem by bringing sales, orders, customers, regional performance, product categories, profit, and shipping-cost metrics into one interactive report.

The analysis can support business teams in:

* Monitoring sales and profit performance
* Comparing regional performance
* Identifying high-performing categories
* Monitoring shipping-related expenses
* Investigating profit drivers
* Finding areas that may require further business review

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. 🏠 Home

The Home page provides an overall view of the e-commerce business.

It includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Profit
* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Regional performance
* Interactive slicers
* Navigation to other report pages

---

### 2. 🌍 Region Analysis

This page focuses on geographical performance.

It analyzes:

* Sales by region
* Profit by region
* Total orders by region
* Total customers
* State-level performance
* Geographic distribution using a map
* Category-based filtering
* Region and state filtering

This helps identify differences in business performance across geographical areas.

---

### 3. 🚚 Shipping Cost Analysis

This page investigates the relationship between shipping costs and business profitability.

Key metrics include:

* Shipping Cost %
* Shipping Cost per Order
* Shipping Cost per Unit
* Profit After Shipping
* Total Sales
* Total Quantity

Visual analysis includes:

* Shipping cost by category
* Sales vs. quantity analysis
* Category-level shipping efficiency analysis

The objective is to identify categories where shipping expenses may have a significant impact on profitability.

---

### 4. 💡 Key Business Insights

The Insights page summarizes important findings from the dashboard.

It focuses on:

* Overall sales and profit performance
* Regional performance
* Shipping-cost impact
* Category concentration
* Profitability considerations
* Potential areas for business improvement

The page converts dashboard analysis into easy-to-understand business observations.

---

### 5. 📈 Profit Driver Analysis

The Profit Driver Analysis page uses a **Decomposition Tree** to investigate profit.

Profit can be broken down using dimensions such as:

* Region
* State
* Category
* Product Description

This allows users to interactively explore which combinations of business dimensions contribute to profit.

## 📌 Key KPIs

| KPI                         | Purpose                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Sales**             | Measures overall sales performance          |
| **Total Orders**            | Tracks order volume                         |
| **Total Customers**         | Measures customer count                     |
| **Profit**                  | Measures overall profitability              |
| **Profit After Shipping**   | Shows profitability after shipping costs    |
| **Shipping Cost %**         | Measures shipping cost relative to sales    |
| **Shipping Cost per Order** | Measures average shipping cost per order    |
| **Shipping Cost per Unit**  | Measures shipping cost relative to quantity |
| **Total Quantity**          | Tracks total quantity sold                  |

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard highlights several areas for business analysis:

* Sales and profit can be concentrated in a smaller number of product categories.
* Regional performance can vary significantly across sales, profit, and order volume.
* Shipping costs can reduce profitability, particularly for categories with higher shipping requirements.
* Shipping cost per order and per unit can be monitored to identify efficiency opportunities.
* Regional concentration can be monitored to understand dependency on specific markets.
* Profit can be investigated further through category, region, state, and product-level breakdowns.

> **Note:** These observations are based on the analysis represented in the Power BI report and should be interpreted together with the selected filters.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Install **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Open the `.pbix` file.
4. Use the slicers to filter the report.
5. Navigate between pages using the report navigation.
6. Explore the visuals to analyze sales, profit, regional performance, and shipping costs.
7. Use the **Profit Driver Analysis** page to drill into the factors contributing to profit.
